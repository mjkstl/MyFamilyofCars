What this app is

My Family of Cars lets a family keep a private, shared record of the cars they have owned, with photos and stories. A family is visible only to the people who create it or join it with an invite.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Names you type for yourself and family members, and how they are relatedTo show your family tree
Cars you add: year, make, model, color, nickname, status, and stories you writeTo build your family's collection
Photos you choose to upload (cars and optional profile photos)To show them to your family
A device sign-in identifier created automatically (no email or password)To keep your family private and tie your entries to you
Your email address, only if you ask to be told when printed keepsakes are availableTo send that notice
Basic usage events (for example "family created", "invite opened") without the content of your entriesTo understand which features are used
The partner or campaign code in a link you arrived from, if anyTo credit the partner who referred you

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. We do not show ads and we do not sell personal information.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores the app's database and photos.
  • Vercel hosts the web version of the app.
  • NHTSA vPIC (a U.S. government vehicle database) receives the make and model year you type, to suggest makes and models. It receives nothing that identifies you.
  • If you tap "Find one online" or "See ones like it for sale", your browser opens a Google search for that car. Google's own privacy policy applies there.

Photos are not sent to any AI service.

Photos

Photos are stored so that the people in your family can view them. Anyone who has the direct web address of a photo can open it, so do not upload photos you would not want shared outside your family.

Sharing

Your family's content is visible to people who join with your invite link or code. You can reset the invite at any time, which stops the old one from working. We disclose information to others only when the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your information until you ask us to delete it. To delete your entries or your whole family, email SUPPORT_EMAIL from the app's Settings screen. We aim to complete requests within 30 days.

Children

The app is meant for adults and families using it together. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent may add a child's name to a family tree; contact us to have it removed.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will update the date above and note it in the app.
