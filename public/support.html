Common questions

How do I invite family? On the Family Tree screen, tap "Invite family", then "Share invite link".

Someone has my invite who should not. Open "Invite family" and tap "Reset invite link". The old link stops working immediately. People who already joined are not removed; email us to remove a member.

How do I change or remove a car? Open the family member, find the car, and use Edit or Delete.

How do I delete my information? In the app, open Settings and choose "Request deletion of my data", or email us. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I report something? Email us with the family name and a description. See the community standards in the Terms of Use.
